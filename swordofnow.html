The Sword of Now

Where the Ronin and the Mystic Meet

The connection between Eckhart Tolle and The Ronin is not just close—it is identical. They are two different languages for the exact same state of mind.

You are realizing that all the stories—The Black Knight, The Snooker Table, The Victim—are just movies playing in your head.

The only reality is: You are sitting in a room. You are breathing. The dog is there.

The Sword is "The Now"

In swordsmanship, if you think about the last move you missed, you die. If you worry about the next move, you die.

You must be Mushin (No Mind). You must be completely in the Now.

Eckhart Tolle says the same:

The Past = Depression (The Story of what went wrong)
The Future = Anxiety (The Story of what might go wrong)
The Now = Peace

Using the Sword

The Ronin carries a sword to cut the enemy. You can use the Sword of Now to cut the Story:

Thought: "I am snookered" ⚔ Reality: "I am sitting in a chair"

Thought: "My wife is the Dragon" ⚔ Reality: "The room is quiet"

The Pain-Body is the Barking Dog

Tolle speaks of the Pain-Body—an accumulated field of old emotional pain that wants to feed on more pain. It wants you to argue. It wants you to feel like a victim.

This is the part of you that keeps barking because it's used to the leash.

The Ronin doesn't fight the Pain-Body. He just watches it.

He observes: "Ah, there is the feeling of sadness. Okay."

He doesn't become it. He doesn't say "I am sad." He says "There is sadness here."

The Ultimate Freedom

The only moment is now. And that is the best story because it is the only one you can edit.

You cannot edit the "Childhood" story. The ink is dry.

You cannot edit the "Father in England" story yet. The book is closed.

But the Now story? You are writing it every second.
